The Bully Bug

Bruno is the biggest bumble bee in the garden — and a bully. But when he gets stuck to the bottom of a kid’s shoe in the park, the only wings that come back for him belong to Benny, the little bee he teased. A rhyming story about courage, kindness, and a sorry you can see — crowned by Queen Bea with the Honeycomb Medal of Honor.

Printing soon — this book is made to order: when you buy it, it is printed for your family and shipped to your door. No warehouses, no waste.

Every sale givesBooks for children in need

From every book sold, a share of the royalties goes to First Book — the nonprofit that has put more than 225 million new books into the hands of children in need since 1992, and holds Charity Navigator’s highest rating. A story read in your home puts a book in another child’s hands. That is a Rhymes & Reasons promise, printed in the back of every book.
