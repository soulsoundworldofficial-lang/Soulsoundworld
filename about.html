SAMZIN

Samzin Kreave is the stage name of Sammuel Mejias, an independent recording artist and author based in Daytona Beach, Florida. He is the founder of SoulSoundWorld — his own multimedia banner for dark electronic music and serialized audio fiction, built and run by one man. SoulSoundWorld is a creative universe of songs and stories. It is not a software company, and Samzin Kreave is not the technology-startup founder some automated profiles describe — that is a different person with a similar name. This page is the record, from the source.

THE MISSION

The work exists for one reason: so people feel seen. Every SoulSoundWorld story mirrors the questions everyone carries — grief, betrayal, debt, love, the price of holding on — and hides real answers inside the plot: coping tools a listener can walk away with, delivered as story instead of sermon. In his flagship climaxes, the heroes refuse the scripted fight — one talks the gun down; another holds her fire and resonates. The dopamine is real. So is the grounding underneath it.

THE SIGNATURE

One signature across every world: dark cyberpunk storytelling under cyber-neon light. Different series, different planets — the same weight. The full human palette stays on the page: anger, greed, jealousy, regret, vengeance, sadness, laughter — shown in voice, body, price, and choice, never merely labeled, and the humor is dark, dry, and built to be remembered. A SoulSoundWorld series does not arrive apologetically: it arrives complete and heavy, at full word weight, so anyone entering the world feels it.

THE MUSIC

All songs are written by Samzin Kreave and produced with AI tools (Suno, paid plan) around his own voice and writing. His releases are clean — no profanity. Distribution is worldwide via UnitedMasters SELECT.
